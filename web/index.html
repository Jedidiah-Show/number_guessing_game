      HOW TO PLAY

      Start small.
      Go as far as you can.

      1. Each level hides a number from 1 to 25 times the level.
      2. Enter a valid whole number. The game tells you whether the secret is higher or lower.
      3. You have ten attempts at each level. Correct answers advance you and earn points.
      4. Correct within five attempts for 5 points; otherwise earn 2. Your run autosaves after every guess.

      Your progress and leaderboards are stored in local JSON files.
